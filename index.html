A pattern worth following

At TelOne, I looked through billing and network data to find where the numbers didn’t line up. The errors I found helped recover more than $10,000. That experience captures what I like about working with data. A discrepancy can look small on a screen and matter a great deal once you know what’s behind it.

I took the same approach into healthcare analytics, where I built Power BI reports to show what was happening across thousands of visits. When I reviewed website traffic, the question changed again. Where were people leaving, and what could we fix? My background in IT, software engineering, and analytics helps me follow a problem from the first clue to a practical next step.
